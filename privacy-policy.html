Privacy Policy

How A-Z Waterproofing and Construction collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: November 2026

1. Who We Are

A-Z Waterproofing and Construction is a building materials supplier based in Kumasi, Ghana. We supply waterproofing products, protective coatings, primers, damp-proof solutions, and related construction materials to homeowners, contractors, and developers across Ghana.

Our website address is azwaterproofing.com. For any privacy-related queries, you may reach us at info@azwaterproofing.com.

2. Information We Collect

When you use our website or contact us, we may collect the following types of information:

Contact Information

Your name, phone number, and email address when you fill out our contact or enquiry form.

Usage Data

Pages visited, time spent on the site, and browser type, collected automatically via cookies and analytics tools.

Email Subscriptions

Your email address if you subscribe to receive product updates, price lists, and promotional communications.

Device & Technical Data

IP address, browser version, operating system, and referring URLs to help us improve site performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your product enquiries and quote requests in a timely manner.
  • To send you email updates about new products, promotions, and pricing changes (only if you subscribed).
  • To analyse site usage and improve the performance and content of our website.
  • To comply with legal obligations under Ghanaian law.
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent or abusive activity on our website.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

4. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies — small text files stored on your device — to enhance your browsing experience. We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic site functionality such as navigation and form submission. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with the site (e.g. page views, bounce rates). We use these to improve the website.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your cookie consent choice so you are not prompted repeatedly.

You may withdraw your consent to non-essential cookies at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the site. Disabling cookies may affect certain site features.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by applicable law. Contact form submissions are retained for a maximum of 24 months. Email subscription data is retained until you unsubscribe.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may link to third-party platforms including WhatsApp, Facebook, and Instagram for customer communication and marketing. These services have their own independent privacy policies. We encourage you to read them before interacting through those channels.

We may use third-party analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand website traffic. These tools may set their own cookies on your device.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your data where we have no legal obligation to retain it.
  • Withdraw consent for email communications at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any email we send.
  • Object to our processing of your data for analytics purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@azwaterproofing.com.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the website after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
