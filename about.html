About A-Z Waterproofing & Construction

Based in Kumasi, Ghana, we supply trusted waterproofing products and construction materials engineered to keep your home safe, dry, and secure during the rainy days.

Our Mission

At A-Z Waterproofing and Construction, our mission is to safeguard Ghanaian buildings against water damage, roof leaks, rising damp, and structural degradation. Ghana's intense rainy seasons demand superior barrier protection, which is why we curate and supply only industry-proven waterproofing systems.

Whether you are an individual homeowner tackling roof leaks, a mason installing D.P.C in foundation walls, or a commercial developer purchasing wholesale Polyurethane Waterproof Paint in bulk, we provide dependable products, competitive pricing, and practical guidance.

Our Core Values

Rainy Season Defense

Specialized waterproofing formulations engineered to endure torrential rains and intense tropical moisture.

Tested Durability

Every bucket of polyurethane paint, primer, and roll of D.P.C undergoes strict testing for long-term bonding and water resistance.

Retail & Wholesale Supply

Whether you need one bucket for home repair or bulk quantities for site construction, we ensure readily available stock in Kumasi.
